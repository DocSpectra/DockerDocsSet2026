## Prerequisites

[Turn on Kubernetes](/manuals/desktop/use-desktop/kubernetes.md#enable-kubernetes) in Docker Desktop.

## Test and debug locally

You can use Docker Desktop to deploy your application to a fully-featured Kubernetes environment on your development machine. This allows you to test and debug your workloads on Kubernetes locally before deploying.

## Deployments and manifests

A Deployment describes a scalable group of identical pods.
