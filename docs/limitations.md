# Known limitations

**One snapshot of one documentation set.** Everything comes from docker/docs at a single commit. The documentation has since changed, and the question IDs, document IDs and judgments apply only to this snapshot.

**Experimental refinements.** LT and R0 are single runs of an automated editor. Some areas were not admitted in a given arm (LT 7 of 25, R0 2 of 25), and the editor was a language model working without human review of its edits. The factual review covered 20 pages per arm. The refinements show what this editing protocol produced; they are not curated documentation, and nothing in the package establishes that they help readers.

**Support judgments are model judgments.** Every support label was produced by one language model judging pooled windows, and only the first stage on the original documentation was spot-checked (70 windows, one reviewer). Pages outside a question's pool are unjudged rather than unsupported, and some judged pages remain undetermined. Measures computed against these qrels inherit the pool, the verifier's leniency (five of 34 sampled `supported` windows were rejected by the reviewer) and the treatment of undetermined pages.

**Version-specific labels.** A refinement can move, split or rewrite a supporting passage, so support for one version does not carry over to another. Each version's qrels were judged on that version's pages. Comparisons across versions should use the per-version files, and the derived-page credit (O1) is a lexical sensitivity measure, not a judgment.

**FAQ-derived questions.** Tier-2 questions are Docker's own FAQ headings, and their reference answers are flattened Markdown. Five question texts occur twice. Questions dropped because no supporting page was found are recorded but may still be answerable from documentation outside the pool.

**Small tier-1 set.** Fifteen tasks with 73 requirements. The requirements were reviewed by the author, but the question wording and support-page lists were not separately reviewed, and the tasks were generated by a language model from page excerpts.

**Recorded model identities.** Model identities are the names recorded at run time. No dated model snapshots were available for the generator, editor, answering or scoring models, so re-running them later may not reproduce the outputs. The package needs no model to load or verify.

**Rendered URLs and anchors.** Upstream links point to repository files at the pinned commit. Rendered documentation-site URLs and heading anchors are approximations or absent.
